## 2. Visual direction and design system

The page MUST be bright, refreshing, colourful, premium and immediately eye-catching. Avoid dark or clinical-looking backgrounds, excessive whitespace above the fold, muted monochrome design, excessive generic icons, cartoon people and repetitive card grids. Use colourful photographic compositions and realistic Malaysian Chinese lifestyle scenes. Alternate full-width photographic sections with clean white content sections. Keep visual hierarchy strong, especially on mobile.
